Privacy Policy

Weather.Trip · Effective November 5, 2026

Short version: you can use Weather.Trip without an account. If you make one, your email is used only to sign you in and keep your saved trips. That's it.

About this project

Weather.Trip is a non-commercial portfolio project. It isn't run by a company, doesn't show ads, and doesn't make money. It's provided as-is, with no guarantee that it will always be available or that forecasts are accurate. Always check official weather alerts before you drive.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and your password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text.
  • Saved trips (signed in only): the start and end you searched, their coordinates, the route distance and drive time, and when you planned it.
  • Guests: if you aren't signed in, your trips are not stored.

How we use it

Your email is used only to identify your account so you can sign in and see your saved trips. We don't send marketing emails, we don't sell or share your email, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

What's stored in your browser

When you sign in, a sign-in token is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in (for up to 7 days). Signing out removes it.

Services that see your searches

To plan a route, the places you search for (never your email) are sent to these services:

  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — turns place names into coordinates and names points on the map
  • OSRM — calculates the driving route
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts along the route, loaded by your browser, so they see your IP address
  • Stadia Maps — map images, loaded by your browser, so they see your IP address

Each has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

While signed in, choose Delete account at the top of the page and re-enter your password. Your account and all of your saved trips are deleted immediately and can't be recovered.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
